## Pick a provider

| Provider               | Cost                  | Data leaves your org? |
| ---------------------- | --------------------- | --------------------- |
| OpenRouter             | Free tier             | Yes                   |
| Google Gemini          | Free tier             | Yes                   |
| Anthropic / OpenAI     | Paid                  | Yes                   |
| 🛡️ Salesforce Einstein | Your org's allocation | **No**                |

Whichever you choose, Apex Doctor only sends a **distilled summary** of the log — never the raw log. Keys are stored in VS Code's encrypted SecretStorage.
